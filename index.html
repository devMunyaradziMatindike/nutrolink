FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our POS solutions

How long does installation take?

Most installations are completed within 1-2 business days. This includes hardware setup, software configuration, and initial testing. Complex multi-location setups may take 3-5 days.

What payment methods are supported?

We support EcoCash, OneMoney, Visa, Mastercard, and cash payments. All payment methods are integrated directly into the POS system for seamless transactions.

Do you provide training?

Yes! We provide comprehensive on-site training for all staff members. Training covers basic operations, inventory management, reporting, and troubleshooting. Additional training sessions are available if needed.

What kind of support do you offer?

We offer 24/7 technical support via phone, WhatsApp, and email. On-site support is available for urgent issues. All systems come with a warranty, and we offer maintenance plans for ongoing peace of mind.

Can I get a custom quote?

Absolutely! Contact us via phone, WhatsApp, or visit our office. We'll assess your business needs and provide a detailed quote with no obligation. We offer flexible payment plans for qualifying businesses.
